# Shift Handoff Pro (planned)

The free page stays free and keeps working on its own. Pro is planned to add:

- **Team live log**: One log shared live across every phone and screen on the team, so everyone sees the same Open, Waiting and Done.
- **Sign-in per person**: Each person signs in, so every entry, move and "I've got it" carries a name you can trust.
- **Stuck-item nudge**: A nudge to the owner, then to the next shift, when an item sits open or waiting longer than you allow.
- **Photo or voice note**: Snap the broken part or leave a short voice note right on the entry.
- **Weekly digest**: A Monday recap of what got done, what keeps coming back and what sat the longest.
- **Spreadsheet export**: A formatted .xlsx workbook with one tab per shift, made on demand or on a schedule.
- **AI shift summary**: A short plain-English handover written from the log, ready to read out at shift change.

Each of these shows as a locked button in the free page, and has a stub in [`src/pro.js`](src/pro.js) that says it is reserved for Pro.
